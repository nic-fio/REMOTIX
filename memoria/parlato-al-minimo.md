Opening **phase 9** (23 Aug 2026): *«In questa sessione assumi un vincolo: riduci il tuo
parlato al minimo. Non mi interessano le spiegazioni tecniche o cosa state facendo tu e gli
agenti: a me interessano i risultati»*.

**Why:** it is the strictest degree of [[parlare-come-al-regista]]. It is not a style preference: the
account of the work in progress — which agents are running, what they are reading, how the
bench is structured — is noise to him. The value is carried by **the outcome**, and by the questions he must decide on.

**How to apply:**
- the report is a few lines: **what has changed** and **what awaits a word from him**;
- no list of what was read, no description of the method, no «now I'll do»;
- the detail does not disappear: it goes **into the documents** (`fasi/NN-*.md`, `DECISIONI.md`), not into the talk;
- the questions stay — one at a time, concrete ([[nic-regista-non-programmatore]]) — because those
  are his decisions, not explanations.
