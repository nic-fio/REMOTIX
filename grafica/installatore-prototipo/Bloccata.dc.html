This version of Debian is not supported

I have not touched anything: the machine is exactly as it was.
Why
REMOTIX uses new-generation technologies that Debian 12 does not have yet: its desktops are too old to be controlled remotely the way REMOTIX does it.
At least Debian 13 is needed. RX-COMPAT-001
What you can do
Upgrade the machine to Debian 13 (Trixie) and restart the installer. The supported versions are listed in the manual.
