Performance report · October 2026

Anyone is fast with one user. REMOTIX stays fast with many.

The same server, the same desktops, the same workload, measured step by step from one user up to sixteen: REMOTIX against xrdp, the remote desktop server most Linux machines use today.

1.8× – 15×more users than xrdp on the same machine, wherever xrdp holds at least one
29 – 30 fpsfor a 30 fps film at every level where all users are green; xrdp drops about 1 frame in 5, even alone
1.5× – 4.6×fewer server CPU cores than xrdp with four users in Full HD: the graphics card does the encoding

One machine: Intel Core i5-13500T, 31 GB RAM, Debian 13. Two graphics cards in turn: the integrated Intel UHD 770 and an AMD Radeon RX 6800. REMOTIX build 716e35b; xrdp 0.10.1 with xorgxrdp 0.10.2, as packaged by Debian 13.

Capacity

How many people can work at the same time

Users who keep working within the limits (latency, video smoothness, input) at each screen size. Every user runs the same mix: typing, scrolling, moving windows, one of them watching a film.

REMOTIXxrdp

16 users is the ceiling of the test bench, not of the card: on the Radeon, at 2K and Full HD, all sixteen users were still green. At 4K on the Radeon, GNOME and KDE hold no one with either product: that is the Vulkan driver shipped by Debian 13 (Mesa 25.0); with Mesa 26 GNOME reaches 4.

⚠
Read the xrdp figures on XFCE and LXQt as a lower bound. xrdp needs, for every session, a block of shared memory as large as the screen. The test boxes had the container default of 64 MB of shared memory, and from there the next xrdp session could not start: 0, 1, 3 and 6 users at 4K, 3K, 2K and Full HD, exactly. On a normal installation that memory is half the RAM and the wall is not there. REMOTIX does not keep the screen in shared memory: in the same boxes, with the same limit, it reached 16 users. The video, CPU and memory figures below do not depend on this.

Method and limits

How it was measured, in short

  • Users are added one at a time, up to 16, at 4K, 3K, 2K and Full HD, on GNOME, KDE Plasma, XFCE and LXQt; each one types, scrolls and moves windows, and one watches a 4K film. A level counts when every user keeps latency under 50 ms and the video at 80% of its frame rate or more.
  • One machine, and a modest one: the Intel card is the integrated one. The clients ran on the server itself, so REMOTIX's figures are a lower bound. xrdp is used as Debian 13 packages it.
  • xrdp is the remote desktop server most Linux machines use today; other products were not measured.
  • Full tables and analysis: the technical manual, chapter «Performance and capacity».
