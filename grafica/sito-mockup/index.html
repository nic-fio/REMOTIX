Use your desktop from any device

The same desktop on a laptop, a tablet or a phone. Your Linux environment, with all its applications and settings, directly in the web browser.

  • Full desktopUse your applications as if you were sitting at the server.
  • Fast, from the graphics cardThe server's GPU encodes the screen; the browser decodes it in hardware.
  • Always where you left itClose the browser, switch device: your windows keep running on the server.
