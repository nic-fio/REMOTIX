B2 — does the WebTransport session open, and does the server answer?

Bench of phase 1. It measures one thing only: whether the engine of this browser establishes a WebTransport session with the given server, accepting its certificate through the fingerprint published here — and whether on that session the server answers, that is whether the HELLO on the control channel gets back an HERE_I_AM (RCP.md §4.2, §4.3). The rest of the handshake belongs to B3, B5 and B11: here the trust model is tested, not the protocol.
